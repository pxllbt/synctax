## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-09

Initial release.

- True black foundation with four surface depth steps
- Electric blue (`#1e66f5`) accent for borders and focus rings
- Four-step text luminance hierarchy (bright `e6e9f0` down to `9ca0b0`)
- Eight high-contrast semantic accent colors
- Three wallpapers with instant cycling
- App coverage across Hyprland, Better Bar, Waybar, GTK/Qt, terminals, Neovim, VSCode
