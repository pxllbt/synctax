## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-09

### Added
- **True Black Foundation**: Engineered 4-step surface depth hierarchy on a pure `#000000` canvas (`#070707`, `#090909`, `#000000`, `#1a1a1a`).
- **Electric Blue Accent**: Single unified accent color (`#1e66f5`) for active Hyprland window borders, selections, and interactive focus states.
- **Measured Text Hierarchy**: 4-step luminance scale (`#e6e9f0`, `#cdd6f4`, `#bcc0cc`, `#9ca0b0`) tested for WCAG legibility on deep black surfaces.
- **Eight Semantic Tokens**: High-contrast semantic accents for syntax highlighting, warnings, errors, links, and notifications.
- **Curated 4K Wallpaper Pack**: Three harmonious backgrounds (`gradient.jpg`, `void.png`, `nebula.png`) with instant wallpaper cycling support.
- **Cross-Desktop Integration**: Comprehensive theme support across Hyprland, Better Bar, Waybar, GTK3/4, Qt5/6, Foot, Kitty, Alacritty, Ghostty, Neovim, and VSCode.
